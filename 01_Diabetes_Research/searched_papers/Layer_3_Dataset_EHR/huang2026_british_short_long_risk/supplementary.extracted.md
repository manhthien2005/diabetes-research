Table S1 Cohort descriptions: Whitehall II (WHII) Study and English Longitudinal Study of Ageing (ELSA)

| Cohort | Descriptions |
| --- | --- |
| Whitehall II (WHII) Study | The WHII study is an ongoing cohort that enrolled 10308 British civil servants (aged 35-55 years; 33.1% women) from 20 London offices in 1985-1988. At enrolment (Phase 1), participants underwent a clinical examination and completed a self-administered questionnaire on demographics, health status, and lifestyle. Subsequent data collection alternated between questionnaire-only phases and phases combining questionnaires with clinical examinations. The WHII received approval from the University College London Research Ethics Committee and adheres to the Declaration of Helsinki. |
| English Longitudinal Study of Ageing (ELSA) | ELSA is a prospective cohort study of adults aged 50 years and older living in the UK, initiated in 2002-2003 (Wave 1) with the recruitment of a nationally representative sample of 11391 participants. Follow-up interviews have been conducted every two years to collect socio-demographic, health, and behavioural information. Nurse visits were conducted every four years starting from Wave 2 (i.e., Waves 2, 4, 6, and 8), during which anthropometric measurements were taken and blood samples collected. ELSA adheres to the Declaration of Helsinki; ethical approval was obtained from the National Research Ethics Service and informed consent from all participants. |

Overall, the WHII study is an occupational cohort of British civil servants. ELSA is a nationally representative cohort of adults aged 50 years and older living in England. Both cohorts collect questionnaire data together with clinical and biomarker measurements.
