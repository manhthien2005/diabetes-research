## Working project title

Feasibility assessment and methodological replication of baseline prediction of incident type 2 diabetes in longitudinal health cohorts.

## Short project description

We propose an academic assessment of whether baseline clinical and questionnaire measurements can support prediction of incident type 2 diabetes over subsequent follow-up. The initial methodological reference is Huang, Lopes and Britton (2026), DOI 10.1111/dom.71289, which develops Cox models in Whitehall II and evaluates them in ELSA. We would first reconstruct cohort eligibility, baseline predictor availability, outcome ascertainment and follow-up using the supplied documentation. Subject to access and data suitability, we would implement Cox-based prediction and the reported predictor-selection approaches, then evaluate discrimination, calibration and decision-analytic performance. Data preprocessing and model selection would be confined to development data, with an independent evaluation reserved after decisions are fixed. The final cohort, outcome definition, predictors, prediction horizons and validation design will be specified after reviewing the available releases and before model fitting. The purpose is methodological research and training; any replication discrepancies and departures from the published methods will be documented.

## Data scope for the feasibility check

- WHII: Phase 3 baseline and follow-up through Phase 9, following the reference paper's framing. ELSA: Wave 2 baseline and follow-up through Wave 10, subject to the release actually available and its licence.
- Anonymous participant linkage identifier and visit order/dates or permitted intervals needed to establish baseline, subsequent outcome and follow-up. No direct identifiers are requested.
- Candidate baseline groups reported in the paper: demographic characteristics; height, BMI and waist measurements; blood pressure; fasting glucose and lipid measures; family history; smoking, alcohol, diet and physical activity; socioeconomic measures; baseline cardiovascular disease and medications.
- Information to identify prevalent diabetes, ascertain subsequent diabetes and distinguish type where supported; last observed follow-up and permitted information on competing death or other end of observation.
- Codebooks, missing-value encodings, laboratory units and fasting status, derived-variable definitions, release history, and sampling/weighting documentation where applicable.

These are conceptual variable groups, not verified codebook mappings. No variable IDs, exact event dates or full cross-cohort equivalence are assumed. Access to linked clinical records, mortality linkage or unarchived variables will require separate assessment of availability and conditions.
